# Code of conduct

HiveScope welcomes people of every background, nationality, language, identity,
ability and level of experience. Be respectful, give specific feedback and make
room for people who are learning. Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.

## Participation rules

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, impersonation, spam
and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.
Do not pressure someone to share hardware, buy tools, reveal identity or use AI.
Credit other people's work and respect consent when sharing conversations or logs.

These rules apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces,
including conduct while representing HiveScope.

## Reporting and moderation

For a non-sensitive public incident, notify the maintainer in the relevant thread
without amplifying abusive content. Use GitHub's **Report content** feature for
harassment or sensitive personal information; do not repost private details in
a public Issue. The project has no separate confidential moderation mailbox.
Security vulnerabilities use the separate [security reporting channel](security.md).

The maintainer may request a correction, issue a warning, hide or remove content,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation according to severity and
repetition. Explain decisions where doing so does not expose private information.
A participant may request reconsideration with relevant evidence in a new,
non-abusive message through an available GitHub channel. Platform enforcement
and appeal processes remain GitHub's responsibility.
